**Version**: 2026-10-10 (effective on the version date)

This policy explains what data GGPlan (subtitle Game Scheduler; "GGPlan" or "we") collects, why we collect it, where it is stored, how long it is kept, who it is shared with, and how you can exercise your rights. The Traditional Chinese version is the official version and other languages are translations; if they differ, the Traditional Chinese version prevails.

> This policy applies to the GGPlan desktop app and the cloud services it uses. You can use GGPlan without signing in; only the features you choose to use collect the corresponding data.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

- **Controller (operator)**: Miku (individual developer), Taiwan.
- **Contact**: there is no dedicated email address yet. Please contact us through "Feedback" in the app: choose the "Other" type and leave contact details we can reply to. Send any questions, requests or complaints about privacy and personal data this way; wherever this policy mentions emailing us or requests made by email, it means this channel. We will update this policy when a dedicated email address is set up.
- **EU representative**: not designated at this time.
- We have not appointed a data protection officer (DPO).

## 2. Summary

- No sign-in required: the first time you launch GGPlan you can choose "Start without signing in" and begin as an **anonymous user**. An anonymous user's ID is a randomly generated code, but because it is linked to your data, we still protect it as personal data.
- Cloud data is stored in Supabase data centers in **Tokyo, Japan**.
- **Usage statistics** are recorded only after you press "Accept", and include nothing you type except the names of private games. You can turn them off in Settings at any time; when you do, the usage statistics already received in the cloud are deleted too.
- We do not sell data, show ads or do personalized marketing, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal effects.
- In the app, under "Settings › Privacy & data", you can **export** all of your data, or **delete** your cloud data and account.

## 3. Data we collect, purposes and legal bases

Legal bases are given under Article 6 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Article 19 of Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). "Performance of a contract" means providing the GGPlan features you ask to use.

### 3.1 Account and sign-in

- **What**: your user ID (a randomly generated UUID), whether you are an anonymous user, and when the user was created and last signed in. When you link Google or Discord, also the account ID, email, display name and avatar URL provided by that service. When you sign in, the cloud service records the time of sign-in, your IP address and device information (the browser's or app's User-Agent), as well as audit logs of sign-ins, sign-outs and similar events.
- **Source**: an anonymous user's data is generated by GGPlan; data for a linked account comes from Google or Discord, where you sign in in your browser (you agree to them providing the data to GGPlan).
- **Purpose**: to identify you, to let you sign in to the same account on other computers, and to keep the service secure.
- **Legal basis**: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); PDPA Art. 19(1)(ii)); sign-in logs are kept for our legitimate interest in service security (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **If you don't provide it**: you can use every feature without signing in, and your data is recognized only on this computer; without a linked account you cannot get your data back on other computers, redeem invite codes or submit profile proposals.

### 3.2 Synced data

- **What**: content you create or change in GGPlan: tracked games (including the mapping when you move to another game), private profiles, changes to routines and resources, completion records (with a random computer ID for each computer), events, resource values, one-time reminders, goals and steps (including back-scheduling settings), and items under "Expired" for which you pressed "Got it". Names, steps and other text in it are typed by you.
- **Purpose**: to sync between this computer and other computers, and to keep your data when you reinstall or switch computers.
- **Legal basis**: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); PDPA Art. 19(1)(ii)).
- **If you don't provide it**: without a cloud connection, your data exists only on this computer.

> When you delete an item, the cloud keeps a "deleted" record (including its last content) so that other computers know to delete it. These records are deleted together with your account.

### 3.3 Plans, invite codes and abuse prevention

- **What**: your plan and its expiry (entitlement records), the invite codes you redeemed and when, the third-party account used to claim a trial (service name and account ID, to make sure each account claims only once), the times invite codes were entered incorrectly, and whether the account has been blocked from submitting profile proposals because of abuse, and why.
- **Purpose**: to provide the plan you obtained; to prevent guessing invite codes, claiming trials more than once and abusive submissions.
- **Legal basis**: performance of a contract (plans); legitimate interest in preventing abuse (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); PDPA Art. 19(1)(ii)).

### 3.4 Usage statistics (requires your consent)

- **What**: which feature you used (e.g. checking things off, using the overlay, adding an event, changing a routine), when you used it, which shared game it was used for (use in private profiles is recorded only as "private"), and some setting values (e.g. layout, theme); and the **names of private games**: the name is recorded once when you create or rename a private profile, and once for each private profile that already exists when you consent (only the name, not the routines, time zone or other contents of the private profile). Records are linked to your user ID. We **do not record** any other text you type (names of routines, events, goals, etc.), your computer information or your game accounts.
- **Consent records**: each time you accept or decline, we record when you answered, the policy version at the time, and whether you confirmed that you are 16 or older (or have permission from a parent or guardian), to demonstrate that we obtained your consent.
- **Purpose**: to understand which features are used most, and decide which features to improve next; the names of private games show how many players have created a profile for the same game themselves, to decide which games to make shared profiles for next.
- **Legal basis**: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); PDPA Art. 19(1)(v)). Consent records are kept to meet the legal obligation to demonstrate consent (GDPR Art. 7(1) and Art. 6(1)(c); PDPA Art. 7(4)).
- **If you decline**: nothing changes; every feature works as usual.
- **Withdrawal**: uncheck "Share usage statistics to help improve GGPlan" in "Settings › Cloud"; it takes a single action, just like giving consent. Once you withdraw, recording stops immediately, usage statistics on this computer that haven't been sent yet are deleted, and usage statistics already received in the cloud are deleted at the next sync. Processing before the withdrawal remains lawful.

### 3.5 Feedback

- **What**: the type you choose (bug report, feature suggestion, other) and the content; optional contact details; and the diagnostic information you did not uncheck: app version, Windows version, the page you were on when sending, and your user ID. To limit how often feedback can be sent, we also record the time it was sent and a hash of its content (deleted after one hour).
- **Purpose**: to understand and fix problems, and to reply to your feedback. Contact details are used only to reply to that feedback.
- **Legal basis**: our legitimate interest in responding to your request and improving GGPlan (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); PDPA Art. 19(1)(v), as you provide it yourself).
- **Note**: feedback sent without your user ID is not linked to your account, so neither export nor account deletion can find it; to have it deleted, email us with the approximate time and content of the feedback. Please don't include unnecessary personal data in your feedback.

### 3.6 Profile proposals (content that becomes public)

- **What**: the game profile you submit (game name, time zone, routines, resources and source links), your note for the maintainers, the ID of this game's private profile on your computer, the review status, the PR number and URL opened by a maintainer, the reason for rejection, and the times of submission and review.
- **Who can see it**: maintainers can see all of it while reviewing, but no identity information other than your account. Once a formal review is decided, a PR with the game profile is opened in GGPlan's GitHub repository; once adopted, it becomes a **shared profile that is public to all players** and is released with the app. **Your account, user ID and note for the maintainers are not made public and are never written into the repository or the PR.**
- **Purpose**: to let more players use a profile for this game.
- **Legal basis**: performance of your request to make it public (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); PDPA Art. 19(1)(ii)). We deliberately do not rely on "consent", because once published the content cannot be fully withdrawn (see below).
- **Cannot be fully deleted once public**: you can withdraw a proposal at any time while it is pending review. Once a maintainer starts the formal review (PR opened), the content stays in GGPlan's version history (git history); even if it is later removed from the current shared profiles, old version history, app versions already released and copies other players have already downloaded may still contain it. So **please don't include any personal information in a proposal** (email, Discord account, phone number, etc.); both the app and the review process check for common formats of personal information, and anything in doubt is not made public.
- **When you delete your account**: your proposal records (including the link between you and your proposals) are deleted, and public content can no longer be linked to you. If you would like us to remove adopted content from the current shared profiles, email us.

### 3.7 Content downloaded from the internet

- **Shared game profiles**: downloaded from the cloud when syncing; they are the same for all players and contain none of your data.
- **App updates**: GGPlan checks for and downloads new versions from GitHub (GitHub Releases of GGPlan-releases). GitHub sees your IP address and connection information and handles them under its own privacy policy; we do not receive this data.

### 3.8 Data stored only on this computer

GGPlan keeps a local copy of all the data above on this computer (by default in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GGPlan`), plus:

- Settings (theme, layout, notifications, overlay, language, startup and close behavior, your usage statistics choice and consent records).
- Records of notifications already sent (to avoid duplicate reminders), tour steps you have seen, and the last version you saw.
- Usage statistics and feedback not sent yet.
- Sign-in credentials: encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), so only the current Windows user on this computer can read them.
- Downloaded shared game profiles and this computer's random ID.

This data is sent only in the situations described in the sections above. Uninstalling GGPlan does not delete this folder; to remove everything, first delete your cloud data in the app (see section 6), then delete this folder.

## 4. Retention periods

- **Account and synced data**: kept until you delete it (by deleting individual items or your account). Anonymous users with no linked account, no data anywhere, and no use for more than 30 days are deleted automatically. **Anonymous users who do have data are deleted automatically, together with all their cloud data, after 24 months without any use** (no sign-in, no sync and no updated data). Anonymous users have no contact details, so **we cannot warn you before this happens**; to keep your data long term, link a Google or Discord account. Linked accounts are never deleted automatically for inactivity.
- **Sign-in audit logs and the cloud platform's connection logs**: not written to the database; kept only in the Supabase platform's short-term logs, for a period that depends on the plan (currently about 1 day).
- **Sessions**: kept until you sign out, the sign-in credentials expire, or you delete your account.
- **Plan, entitlement and trial records**: kept until you delete your account.
- **Records of invite code errors and feedback submissions**: deleted after 1 hour.
- **Usage statistics**: deleted 13 months after the server receives them; deleted immediately when you withdraw consent or delete your account.
- **Consent records**: kept until you delete your account.
- **Feedback**: deleted 1 year after it is sent; contact details are deleted first, after 90 days.
- **Profile proposals**: while pending review (including PR opened), kept until the review ends; proposal records are deleted 180 days after withdrawal, rejection or adoption; deleted immediately when you delete your account. For content that has been made public, see section 3.6.
- **Data on this computer**: kept until you delete it in the app, sign out (which clears the player data), or delete the folder.

Our current cloud plan has no database backups, so deleted data does not linger in backups. If we enable backups later, we will update this policy and state how long backups are kept.

## 5. Where data is stored and who it is shared with

We do not sell or rent your data. Only the following parties have access to data:

- **Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore)**: processor for the cloud database, sign-in and server functions; data is stored in Tokyo, Japan (the ap-northeast-1 region of Amazon Web Services). Supabase processes data under its data processing agreement (DPA), which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **GitHub (GitHub, Inc., United States)**: hosts GGPlan's source code and the PRs for profile proposals, and provides app update downloads.
- **Google, Discord**: only when you choose to sign in or link with them, in which case you interact with them directly in your browser; they handle data under their own privacy policies.
- **All players**: only the content of game profiles from adopted profile proposals (see section 3.6).
- **Authorities**: only when required by law.

**International transfers**: your data is transferred to Japan (where it is stored) and processed in Singapore and the United States, where our processors are located. Japan is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission; other transfers rely on safeguards such as the processors' Standard Contractual Clauses. For users in Taiwan: this is an international transfer as defined in the Personal Data Protection Act, and no competent authority currently restricts transfers to these regions.

## 6. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under GDPR Articles 15 to 22 and PDPA Article 3, you have the following rights. The rights under PDPA Article 3 cannot be waived in advance or limited by agreement.

- **Inquiry, review and copies, data portability**: in "Settings › Privacy & data", press "Export my data" to download a JSON file containing all your cloud data (account, linked identities, synced data, plan, usage statistics, consent records, feedback sent with your user ID, profile proposals) and the data on this computer (except sign-in credentials).
- **Correction and supplementation**: most data can be changed directly in the app. For other data, email us.
- **Deletion**: in "Settings › Privacy & data", press "Delete my cloud data and account" and confirm twice; all your data and your user in the cloud are deleted immediately, the player data on this computer is cleared as well (as when signing out; settings are kept), and you return to the welcome page. Deletion cannot be undone. If you use the same account on other computers, sign out on those computers first; otherwise, at their next sync they will upload their own data again as a new anonymous user. For profile proposal content that has been made public, see section 3.6; for feedback sent without your user ID, see section 3.5.
- **Withdrawing consent**: uncheck usage statistics in "Settings › Cloud" (see section 3.4).
- **Restriction of processing, objection, and stopping collection or use**: email us. You can object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Complaints**: you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live or work (for example, a data protection authority of an EU member state). Users in Taiwan can raise concerns with the competent authority for personal data protection.

**Response times**: export and deletion in the app are completed immediately. For requests made by email, we reply within 15 days to requests for inquiry, review and copies, and within 30 days to other requests; if necessary, we may extend the period once and will tell you why (under the GDPR the period is at most 1 month, extendable by 2 months where necessary). Exercising your rights is free of charge in principle.

**Verifying your identity**: to prevent others from impersonating you, export and deletion in the app apply only to the user currently signed in on this computer. When you make a request by email, we may ask you to provide your user ID ("Settings › Account" shows the first 8 characters; the full ID is in the exported file), or to email us from the email address of your linked account. Anonymous users can only act from the app on this computer; we cannot verify an anonymous user's identity through other channels.

## 7. Minors

- GGPlan is not a service designed for children.
- Consent to **usage statistics** is accepted only from users who are 16 or older, or who have permission from a parent or guardian; you must check this confirmation to consent. If you are under 16 and don't have permission from a parent or guardian, please choose "Decline".
- In Taiwan, users under 18 generally need permission from their legal representative (usually a parent) to give consent or make other declarations of intent. Please read this policy together with a parent or guardian.
- If you are a parent or guardian and find that your child provided data without permission, email us and we will delete the related data.

## 8. Security measures

- All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
- The cloud database is protected by row-level access control: each player can only read and write their own data.
- The app contains only public keys; the administrative key that can read and write all data exists only on the server side and never appears in the app or the source code.
- Sign-in credentials on this computer are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI).
- We collect only the data needed to provide features; usage statistics contain nothing you type except the names of private games, and submitted profile proposals are checked for personal information.

If a personal data breach occurs that may affect you, we will notify the authorities as required by law and inform affected players in an appropriate way.

## 9. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the version date above and explain the change in what's new. If a change is significant, such as one to the data collected for usage statistics, its purposes, recipients or retention periods, GGPlan asks for your consent again the next time you open it; no usage statistics are recorded until you answer. Other significant changes are announced in the app.

You can read this policy in GGPlan at any time ("Privacy policy" in the manual, no internet connection needed), or read the [online version](https://github.com/Miku4364/GGPlan-releases/blob/main/privacy/en.md).
